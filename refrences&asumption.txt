##Assumptions common to all models

Initial Conditions & Kick Tracking:
 - Initial position (t = 0.00s): (0, 0, 0.11) m (treated as exact).
 - Observed positions provided at t = 0.05s and t = 0.10s (treated as exact).
 - Launch velocity is estimated by fitting the kinematic model to these two positions.

Ball & Environment Properties:
   Point mass approximation for translational motion.
   Mass (m): 0.43 kg
   Radius (r): 0.11 m
   Gravity (g): 9.81 m/s^2 (constant, pointing downward).

Goal-Plane Prediction & Constraints:
   Target goal plane: x = 11.0 m (evaluates position of the ball's center at crossing).
   Ignores ground collisions, post/crossbar impacts, and physical ball size upon crossing.

Monte Carlo Noise & Uncertainty Analysis:
   Initial position stays fixed at (0, 0, 0.11) m.
   Independent zero-mean Gaussian noise (std dev = 5 mm / 0.005 m) is added to each coordinate of the t = 0.05s and t = 0.10s observations.
   - Noise level is assumed for tracking uncertainty propagation only; does not account for external physical variations or real-world accuracy.


##Main variable-aerodynamics model: drag only

The ball moves through stationary air; wind and indoor air currents are neglected.

Air density is approximated using the selected Kathmandu-altitude model and changes with ball height according to that model.

Drag force opposes the ball’s air-relative velocity, and its magnitude is proportional to speed squared.

The drag coefficient varies with speed according to the empirical relation implemented in the model.

No Magnus force is included in this version.


##Variable-Aerodynamics Model (Drag + Magnus Force):
   
 Combines the variable drag coefficient C_D(v) and atmospheric density assumptions from the drag-only model with Magnus force.
  
 Magnus lift force is computed using an empirical lift-coefficient relation.
   
 Prescribed spin vector: (5, 10, 32) rad/s (assumed constant in both magnitude and direction throughout flight).
  
 Spin is an assumed parameter, not measured or fitted from the observed position tracking data.
   
 Neglects spin decay, aerodynamic wobble, seam/panel asymmetries, and dynamic atmospheric changes.


##Constant-Coefficient Quadratic-Drag Baseline:
   - Drag acceleration: a_D = -q * |v| * v (drag force acts opposite to velocity, magnitude proportional to speed squared).
   - Coefficient (q): Assumed constant throughout flight; fitted along with initial launch velocity to the two observed positions (t = 0.05s and t = 0.10s).
   - Exclusions: Does not include a speed-dependent drag coefficient C_D(v), altitude-dependent air density, or Magnus force.
   - Limitation: Fitted coefficient and resulting trajectory predictions are strictly conditional on this simplified drag model.

##Linear-Drag Baseline:
   - Drag acceleration: a_D = -k * v (drag force acts opposite to velocity, magnitude proportional to speed, linear velocity dependence).
   - Coefficient (k): Assumed constant throughout flight; fitted along with initial launch velocity to the two observed positions (t = 0.05s and t = 0.10s).
   - Exclusions: Simplified comparison model; excludes Magnus force, speed-dependent drag coefficient C_D(v), and altitude-dependent air density.
